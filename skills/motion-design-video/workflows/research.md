# Research

Understand the subject well enough to visualize it accurately. Research whenever accurate subject
knowledge is necessary.

## Identify

Starting from the creative brief, identify:

- The central message.
- The intended audience.
- The essential concepts.
- The most interesting visual opportunities.
- The appropriate level of detail.
- The relationships that can be communicated through motion.
- The elements that should remain consistent throughout the video.

Determine what the viewer should understand, remember, or feel.

## Verify

If the subject involves factual claims, verify them using reliable sources.

Classify every claim the video will make as one of:

- Established fact.
- Simplified explanation.
- Conceptual metaphor.
- Implementation detail.
- Speculative interpretation.

Do not fabricate technical behavior, scientific mechanisms, historical facts, or product
capabilities. Accuracy should inform visual design without unnecessarily restricting creativity.

## Output

Research notes containing:

- Verified claims with their sources and classifications.
- The essential concepts at the depth the audience needs.
- A shortlist of relationships that motion can reveal.
