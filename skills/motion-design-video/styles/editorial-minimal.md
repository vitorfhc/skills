# Editorial Minimal

Precise geometry on a dark field. Lines become shapes, shapes become diagrams, and nothing moves
without a reason. It looks like a well-set technical journal brought to life.

## Fits

- Good for: technical explainers, systems and processes, developer tools, conceptual pieces, and
  any subject where clarity is the show.
- Weak for: playful consumer brands, hype launches, and subjects that need warmth or physical
  texture.

## Look

| Role | Dark | Light |
|------|------|-------|
| Background | `#07080A` | `#F4F4F1` |
| Primary text | `#F5F5F5` | `#0B0C0E` |
| Structural lines | `#343841` | `#C9CBD1` |
| Primary accent | `#1B3ED2` | `#1B3ED2` |
| Active elements | `#45F0DD` | `#0E9C8E` |
| Secondary emphasis | `#FFB547` | `#C77800` |
| Positive outcomes | `#91F2A3` | `#1E8C3A` |
| Errors or contrast | `#FF6174` | `#D22B45` |

Typography is Geist for titles and text, and Geist Mono for numbers, code, measurements, and
technical annotations.

| Element | Weight | Treatment |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Major titles | 900 | Large, commanding |
| Secondary headings | 700–800 | Clear hierarchy |
| Labels | 600–800 | Compact, uppercase, generous tracking |
| Supporting text | 500–600 | Highly legible |
| Technical values | 500–700 | Monospaced |

Compose with editorial restraint: a strict grid, purposeful negative space, clean vector strokes of
consistent weight, and technical illustration where it helps.

## Motion

- Transform rather than replace: lines becoming shapes, shapes becoming letters, letters becoming
  diagrams, diagrams becoming interfaces, numbers becoming geometry.
- Transitions are shape morphs, geometric unfolding, path transformations, mask reveals, and
  spatial reorganization.
- Easing is precise with no overshoot: a critically damped spring for positioning, exponential
  in/out for wipes and exits, linear for continuous motion, and stepped for discrete state changes.
- The camera is locked, front-facing, and orthographic. Objects move and the canvas does not.

## Rendering

SVG or 2D canvas. Keep strokes crisp and avoid textures.

## Embraces and Breaks It

- Embraces: hairline structure, exact alignment, numbers interpolating between values, labels
  emerging from objects, and one confident typographic moment.
- Breaks it: particle bursts, glow, lens flares, camera shake, RGB splitting, decorative gradients,
  bounce, glitch, motion blur, arbitrary 3D, and decorative noise.

## Dials

- **Field:** dark, or light paper.
- **Accent:** the default blue and cyan, or the brand's colors mapped onto the same roles.
- **Density:** a sparse stage with one object, or a fully annotated diagram.

## Pitfalls

- Sterility: give the film a motif that evolves and at least one bold typographic moment.
- Vanishing hairlines: keep structural lines at least 2 px thick at a short side of 1080.
